# Civil War West Point Resources

## Knowledge

### Books

- [Book: *The Class of 1846: From West Point to Appomattox* — John C. Waugh](https://johncwaugh.com/home/bibliography/the-class-of-1846-from-west-point-to-appomattox-stonewall-jackson-george-mcclellan-and-their-brothers/)
  The definitive book on West Point's most famous class. Covers Jackson, McClellan, Pickett, Hill, and their classmates from cadet days to Civil War battlefields. Winner of the Fletcher Pratt Award. Use for: the Class of '46 deep dive, the McClellan-Hill-Marcy love triangle, Pickett's last-in-class story.

- [Book: *Sacred Ties: From West Point Brothers to Battlefield Rivals* — Tom Carhart](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/7852222-sacred-ties)
  Follows six West Point classmates — Custer, Pelham, Merritt, Ramseur, Rosser, and DuPont — who fought on opposite sides. A West Point graduate and army historian, Carhart brings insider access. Use for: the cavalry commanders' web, Custer's friendships with Confederates.

- [Book: *Civil War Generals: Comrades, Peers, Rivals — In Their Own Words* — Robert Girardi](https://www.defensemedianetwork.com/stories/book-review-civil-war-generals-comrades-peers-rivals-in-their-own-words/)
  Uses the "slam book" format — what generals wrote about each other. Captures jealousies, rivalries, and character judgments in their own words. Use for: primary-source personality sketches, the "gossip" angle.

- [Book: *The West Point History of the Civil War* — The United States Military Academy](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-West-Point-History-of-the-Civil-War/The-United-States-Military-Academy/The-West-Point-History-of-Warfare-Series/9781476782621)
  Beta-tested by actual cadets. Authoritative but accessible. Good for understanding what West Point was like in the 1850s — the sectional divisions, the brawls, the political tension. Use for: the atmosphere at the academy pre-war, Emory Upton abolitionist story.

- [Book: *The Training Ground: Grant, Lee, the Mexican War, and the Gateway to the Civil War* — Martin Dugard](https://www.worldhistory.org/article/2867/civil-war-generals-in-the-mexican-american-war/)
  Covers how the Mexican-American War served as a training ground and social network for future Civil War commanders. Use for: understanding the pre-war bonds formed in combat, the Mexico "reunion."

### Articles

- [Article: "The Key Way West Point Prepared Ulysses S. Grant for the Civil War" — Elizabeth D. Samet (History.com)](https://www.history.com/articles/ulysses-s-grant-civil-war-west-point)
  Written by a West Point professor. Covers Grant's relationships with 50+ future generals he met at the academy, his friendship with Longstreet, and the Buckner surrender story. Use for: Grant's social network, his personality sketches of opponents.

- [Article: "Classmates Divided" — American Heritage (Feb 1958)](https://www.americanheritage.com/classmates-divided)
  Beautifully written piece on the classes of 1861 — the last pre-war classes. Captures the tragedy, the love between enemies, the story of Edmund Kirby dying with Lincoln at his bedside. Use for: the emotional core, the "love your enemy" angle.

- [Article: "West Point and the Civil War" — Richard Lawson Singley (Medium)](https://richardsingley.medium.com/west-point-and-the-civil-war-8abe30451a9d)
  Broad survey covering multiple classes, key friendships (McPherson-Hood, Custer-Washington, Grant-Buckner), and the Lee-Davis vs Lee-Johnston dynamics. Use for: quick reference, multiple story threads in one article.

### Other Media

- [YouTube: "West Point Classmates Become Civil War Enemies - Custer and Washington"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xjPIfJAgd3c)
  Visual storytelling of the famous photo showing Custer with his captured classmate. Use for: the iconic image, visual reinforcement.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/CivilWar](https://reddit.com/r/CivilWar) — Active community, good for asking questions and finding book recommendations.
- [CivilWarTalk Forums](https://civilwartalk.com) — Long-running forum with knowledgeable members. Use for: specific deep-dive questions, book suggestions.
- [Emerging Civil War](https://emergingcivilwar.com) — Blog with regular historian contributors. Good for book reviews and personality-focused articles.

## Gaps

- No single resource yet found that focuses purely on *personal relationships* (not battles). The Girardi book comes closest. The "reality TV" angle may need to be synthesized from multiple sources.

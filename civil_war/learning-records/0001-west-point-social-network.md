# 0001 — West Point as a Social Network

Established the framing for this learning journey: the Civil War understood through the personal relationships forged at West Point (1828–1861). The user is a novice but wants a storytelling, "reality TV" approach — character-driven, not battle-driven.

Key framing accepted: West Point was a tiny, isolated institution where ~250 cadets lived together for four years, creating an intimate social network that later became the Civil War officer corps. Five major relationship arcs identified: (1) McClellan-Hill-Marcy love triangle, (2) Grant-Longstreet friendship, (3) Armistead-Hancock at Gettysburg, (4) Lee-Johnston-Davis feud, (5) Custer-Rosser cavalry circle.

# 0006 — Module 2: Grant's Web (Class of 1843)

Created Lesson 2 following the expert team's 6-phase structure: Hook → Storytelling → Knowledge Injection → Connection Map → Retrieval Practice → Preview.

Covers Grant's reluctant arrival at West Point (1839), the origin of his name (Hiram Ulysses → "U.S." by paperwork error), his mediocrity as a student vs brilliance as a horseman, and three key friendships: Longstreet (best man at his wedding), Buckner (surrendered at Fort Donelson), and Sherman (his most trusted commander).

Also debunks the Grant-as-drunkard myth as a trick clue.

Retrieval practice includes: 4 fill-the-blank warmups, 5 Core Jeopardy clues, and 3 "Previously On…" spaced questions from Module 1. This is the first lesson with the spaced-repetition recall section.

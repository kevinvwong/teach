# Mission: West Point Generals of the Civil War

## Why
A history buff deepening their understanding of the Civil War — not as a dry sequence of battles, but as a human drama. The goal is to *feel* the war through the personal relationships forged (and fractured) at West Point: friendships, rivalries, mentoring, and the tragedy of classmates turned enemies.

## Success looks like
- **Jeopardy ready** — can answer any clue about West Point connections between Civil War generals
- Can name the key West Point classes and their major figures (1846, 1843, 1829, etc.)
- Can tell the stories of specific friendships/rivalries (Grant-Longstreet, Armistead-Hancock, McClellan-Hill, Lee-Johnston)
- Can explain how pre-war relationships shaped battlefield decisions
- Can recommend one great book on the topic to another history buff

## Constraints
- Self-directed learning, no deadlines
- Prefers storytelling over dry facts — "reality TV style"
- Novice level — start with overview, build depth over sessions

## Out of scope
- Detailed battle tactics or campaign strategies (focus is the *people*)
- Post-Civil War careers except where they illuminate relationships
- Political history of the 1850s except where needed for context

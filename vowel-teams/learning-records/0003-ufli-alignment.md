# 0003 — UFLI Alignment

Aligned the vowel teams course to the [UFLI Foundations](https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/) scope and sequence (Lessons 84–97). This replaces the original ad-hoc module order with UFLI's research-backed progression:

- **Unit 1: Long Vowel Teams** (UFLI 84-88): ai/ay → ee/ea/ey → oa/ow/oe → ie/igh → Review
- **Unit 2: Other Vowel Teams** (UFLI 89-94): oo → ew/ui/ue → Review → au/aw/augh → ea/ĕ, a/ŏ
- **Unit 3: Diphthongs** (UFLI 95-97): oi/oy → ou/ow → Final Review

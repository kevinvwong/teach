# Vowel Teams Resources

## Knowledge

### Primary

- **[UFLI Foundations Toolbox](https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/foundations/toolbox/)** — Free, research-based phonics program from the University of Florida Literacy Institute. Lessons 84–97 cover all vowel teams and diphthongs. Includes lesson slide decks, decodable passages, and word work mats. Use for: scope and sequence gold standard, lesson activities, decodable texts.

- **[UFLI Foundations Scope and Sequence (PDF)](https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/UFLI-Scope2.pdf)** — The complete scope and sequence for all 128 UFLI lessons. Use for: understanding where vowel teams fit in the broader phonics progression.

- **[UFLI Virtual Teaching Resource Hub](https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/resources/teaching-resources/)** — Free digital tools including blending board, word work mats, and heart word cards. Use for: interactive practice activities.

### Supporting

- [Article: OG for All — How to Teach Vowel Team Syllables](https://ogforall.com/how-to-teach-vowel-team-syllables-for-the-best-outcome/)
  Orton-Gillingham based guide. Explains the systematic progression for teaching vowel teams. Use for: lesson sequence, teaching methodology.

- [Article: We All Can Read — Orton-Gillingham Principles & Letter Order](https://weallcanread.com/orton-gillingham-principles/)
  Complete scope and sequence showing where vowel teams fit in the OG progression. Use for: understanding what comes before and after vowel teams.

- [Article: Spelling Test Buddy — UFLI Scope and Sequence Guide](https://spellingtestbuddy.com/resources/ufli-scope-and-sequence/)
  Practical walkthrough of all UFLI phases with word lists. Use for: lesson planning, word list generation.

- [Article: Literacy Learn — Free Phonics Scope & Sequence](https://literacylearn.com/free-phonics-scope-sequence/)
  Research-backed scope and sequence chart. Use for: supplementing UFLI with additional practice.

### Key Differentiators

1. **Age-appropriate for older struggling readers** — Most phonics resources max out at Grade 2. Our course is designed specifically for 9-11 year olds who need remediation, with a tone and visual style that respects their developmental stage (not "babyish").

2. **Self-paced, no account needed** — Lessons open in any browser. No login, no data collection, no paywall. Students can work independently.

3. **UFLI-aligned with interactive practice** — Combines the research-backed UFLI scope and sequence with interactive elements (checkboxes, text inputs, `<details>` toggles) that are missing from UFLI's free resources.

4. **Image-rich, print-quality design** — Each lesson includes hero images from Pexels, vowel team highlighting, and a clean Tufte-inspired layout that prints well.

5. **No JavaScript dependencies** — All interactivity works with pure HTML/CSS (`<details>`, `<input>`), making lessons accessible on any device including older computers and school Chromebooks.

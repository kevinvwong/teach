# Mission: Vowel Teams

## Why
Ages 9-11 students need remediation on vowel teams — two vowels working together to make one sound. They know basic phonics but struggle with these more complex patterns, affecting reading fluency and spelling.

## Success looks like
- Student can read and spell words with common vowel teams (ai, ay, ee, ea, igh, oa, oe, ue, ui, oi, oy, ou, ow, au, aw, oo)
- Student can choose the correct vowel team spelling when writing
- Student can read multisyllabic words containing vowel teams
- Student gains confidence in reading and writing

## Constraints
- Remediation setting — students have been exposed before but need systematic re-teaching
- Ages 9-11 — needs to feel age-appropriate, not "babyish"
- Keep lessons short and game-like (15-20 min each)

## Out of scope
- Basic letter sounds (assumed known)
- CVC words (assumed known)
- Consonant digraphs (sh, ch, th, wh — assumed known)
- Silent-e syllables (assumed known)

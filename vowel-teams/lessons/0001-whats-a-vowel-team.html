Starting Strong

What's a Vowel Team?

A vowel team is when two vowels work together to make one sound. Instead of each vowel saying its own sound, they team up and share the job.

Think of it like a seesaw. Two kids sit on a seesaw, but only one goes up while the other goes down. They move together as one. That's how vowel teams work — two vowels that act as one sound.

Here are four common vowel teams you'll see all the time:

ai as in rain
ay as in play
ee as in see
ea as in eat
oa as in boat
ow as in snow
oo as in moon
igh as in night

Key Idea

When you see two vowels side by side in a word, they might be a vowel team. Say the first vowel's long sound and see if that fits. Not every pair of vowels is a team — sometimes they split into separate syllables — but this is a great place to start.

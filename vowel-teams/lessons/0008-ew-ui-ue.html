Module 8

ew, ui, and ue

UFLI Lesson 91

The long U sound can be spelled ew, ui, or ue. Three ways to make the same sound!

Three Spellings, One Sound

You already know the long U sound from oo. Now let's learn three more spellings that make the same sound.

ue at the End

When you hear the long U sound at the end of a word, it's often spelled ue.

blue   true   glue   clue   value

ew at the End

The long U sound at the end can also be spelled ew.

grew   flew   drew   new   blew

ui in the Middle

When the long U sound appears in the middle of a word, it may be spelled ui.

fruit   suit   juice   cruise   bruise

Memory trick: ue and ew compete at the end of words. If it's not a word you know, try ue first (it's more common). ui almost always appears in the middle — you'll rarely see it at the end!

Quick Check

  1. Which vowel team is in the word "true"? ue
  2. Which vowel team is in the word "grew"? ew
  3. Spell the word: /fruit/ (a sweet food from trees). fruit

Well done! You now know three more spellings for the long U sound: ew, ui, and ue.

Long O: oa, ow, oe

UFLI Lesson 86

The long O sound can be spelled three ways. Let's find them all!

Three Ways to Spell /o/

When you hear the long O sound in a word, look carefully. It might be spelled oa, ow, or oe. Each spelling has its own job.

oa — in the middle

oa usually comes in the middle of a word or syllable. You won't find it at the end.

boat road soap goal

ow — at the end (says /o/)

ow can say /o/ at the end of a word. (Watch out — ow can also say /ou/ like in cow! That's a different lesson.)

snow grow slow know

oe — at the end (rare)

oe is a less common spelling. It always comes at the end of a word.

toe doe hoe foe

Quick tip: If the /o/ sound is in the middle, try oa first. If it's at the end, try ow first. oe is less common but good to know.

Game: Fill the Blank

Read each sentence. Which word with oa, ow, or oe fits? Say your answer aloud, then click to check.

1. The car drove down the _____.

Choices: road, roast, coat

Show answer

road — The car drove down the road.

2. The deer and the _____ ate grass in the field.

Choices: toe, doe, hoe

Show answer

doe — The deer and the doe ate grass in the field.

3. We watched the _____ fall from the sky.

Choices: snow, grow, flow

Show answer

snow — We watched the snow fall from the sky.

4. Don't forget to wash your hands with _____.

Choices: goal, soap, coach

Show answer

soap — Don't forget to wash your hands with soap.

5. Plants need sunlight and water to _____.

Choices: throw, blow, grow

Show answer

grow — Plants need sunlight and water to grow.

6. She stubbed her _____ on the chair leg.

Choices: toe, doe, foe

Show answer

toe — She stubbed her toe on the chair leg.

Quick Check

  1. Which vowel team goes in the middle of a word like b__t? oa
  2. Which vowel team goes at the end of a word like sn__? ow
  3. Which vowel team is the rarest of these three and always comes at the end? oe

Great job! You now know three ways to spell the long O sound: oa in the middle, ow at the end, and oe at the end (rare).

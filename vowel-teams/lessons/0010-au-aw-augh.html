au, aw, augh — The /aw/ Sound

UFLI Lesson 93

The /aw/ sound can be spelled au, aw, or augh. Three different spellings for one sound!

Three Ways to Spell /aw/

When you hear the /aw/ sound (like in saw), there are three ways to spell it. Where the sound appears in the word tells you which spelling to use.

au — Usually in the Middle

Use au when the /aw/ sound is in the middle of a word or syllable.

sauce August fault launch cause

aw — Usually at the End

Use aw when the /aw/ sound is at the end of a word or syllable.

saw draw straw claw law

augh — The Tricky One

augh is a less common spelling. It always says /aw/.

caught taught naughty daughter slaughter

Tip: If you're not sure, try au first. It is the most common spelling for the /aw/ sound.

Module 11

ea Says /ĕ/, a Says /ŏ/

UFLI Lesson 94

Sometimes ea says short e, and sometimes the letter a says short o! English is full of surprises.

Two Sound Surprises

You already know that ea can say long e (like in eat). But ea has another job — it can also say short e, like in bread.

And here's another surprise: The letter a can sometimes say /ŏ/ (short o). We see this in a small group of common words.

ea says /ĕ/

In some words, ea makes the short e sound instead of long e. You just have to remember which ones!

bread head read (past tense) weather dead

a says /ŏ/

In some common words, the letter a makes the short o sound. This only happens before certain letters.

want wash watch water what

Why does this happen? The /w/ sound pulls the a toward the /ŏ/ sound in words like want, wash, and watch. Your mouth is already rounded from the /w/, so a sounds more like o.

Read These Words

Read each word aloud. Be careful — these words don't sound the way they look!

bread I made a sand-bread and jelly sandwich.
head She wore a hat on her head.
read Yesterday I read three chapters.
weather The weather is cold and rainy today.
want I want a slice of pizza.
watch Did you watch the game last night?
wash Don't forget to wash your hands.
water The water in the pool is warm.

Odd One Out

In each group, three words share a sound pattern. One word is different. Can you find the odd one out?

🕵️ Odd One Out

Say each word aloud. Which word doesn't belong?

  1. Set 1

    bread head dead bean
    Show answer

    bean is the odd one out! Bread, head, and dead use ea = /ĕ/. Bean uses the long e sound.

  2. Set 2

    want wash water wake
    Show answer

    wake is the odd one out! Want, wash, and water use the /ŏ/ sound for a. Wake uses the long a sound.

  3. Set 3

    read (present) seat team meat
    Show answer

    No odd one out here! Read (present tense), seat, team, and meat all use ea = long e. But watch out — read can also be past tense with /ĕ/!

Quick Check

  1. What sound does ea make in "bread"? short e (/ĕ/)
  2. What sound does a make in "want"? short o (/ŏ/)
  3. Name one word where ea says short e. bread, head, dead, weather, read (past tense), and many more

Excellent! You now know two of English's trickiest sound surprises. Keep practicing these words — the more you see them, the easier they get.

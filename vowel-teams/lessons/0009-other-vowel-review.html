Summary: Other Vowel Teams

Here's everything we've learned so far about the "other vowel teams."

Long /ū/ oo moon, food, spoon
Short /ŭ/ oo book, look, good
End /ū/ ue blue, true, glue
End /ū/ ew grew, flew, drew
Middle /ū/ ui fruit, suit, juice

Key things to remember:

  • oo can be long (moon) or short (book) — learn the word families.
  • ue and ew both spell /ū/ at the end of words.
  • ui spells /ū/ inside a word, not at the end.

Quick Check

  1. Which vowel team spells the /ū/ sound in "grew"? ew
  2. Does "book" use the long or short sound of oo? Short /ŭ/
  3. Does ui usually appear at the end or the middle of a word? The middle
  4. Spell the word: /flood/ (something you eat). food
  5. Which two vowel teams both make the /ū/ sound at the end of a word? ue and ew

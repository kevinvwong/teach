ou and ow — The /ou/ Sound

UFLI Lesson 96

The /ou/ sound — just think of a loud noise! Cloud, mouth, cow.

ou in the Middle, ow at the End

The /ou/ sound (like in shout) follows a very common pattern. And here's a twist: ow can also say /ō/ (long o), like in snow. Let's see both!

ou — Usually in the Middle

Use ou when the /ou/ sound is in the middle of a word or syllable.

cloud out mouth loud count

ow — Usually at the End

Use ow when the /ou/ sound is at the end of a word or syllable.

cow down brown now how

ow Can Also Say /ō/

Here's the tricky part: ow can also say long o, like in snow. You have to try both and see which sounds right!

snow (/ō/) grow (/ō/) know (/ō/) cow (/ou/) down (/ou/)

Read These Words

Read each word aloud. Decide: does ow say /ou/ or /ō/?

cloud A big cloud covered the sun.
out The cat wants out of the house.
mouth Open your mouth wide for the dentist.
loud The music was too loud!
count Can you count to 100?
cow The cow grazed in the field.
down Please sit down.
brown The brown dog wagged its tail.
now What should we do now?
how How did you do that?

Sound Sort — /ou/ or /ō/?

Some ow words say /ou/ and some say /ō/. Can you sort them?

🔊 Sound Sort

Say each ow word aloud. Does it make the /ou/ sound or the /ō/ sound?

cow snow down grow brown know how show now blow
Show answers

/ou/ cow, down, brown, how, now — these ow words make the /ou/ sound.

/ō/ snow, grow, know, show, blow — these ow words make the long o sound.

Tip: Short words like cow, how, now usually use /ou/. Longer words with other consonants often use /ō/.

Quick Check

  1. Which spelling of /ou/ usually goes at the end of a word? ow
  2. Which spelling of /ou/ usually goes in the middle of a word? ou
  3. Does "snow" use the /ou/ sound or the /ō/ sound? /ō/ (long o)

Fantastic! You now know the /ou/ sound spelled ou and ow, plus you learned that ow can say /ō/ too. That's a lot of brain power!

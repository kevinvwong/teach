Vowel Team Overview

Warm-up & Introduction

Vowel teams are everywhere. Can you spot them?

What's a Vowel Team?

A vowel team is when two vowels work together to make one sound. They share the job.

There are lots of vowel teams. Let's start with four common pairs.

Long A — ai / ay

ai rain ay play

Long E — ee / ea

ee see ea eat

Long O — oa / ow / oe

oa boat ow snow oe toe

Two Sounds — oo

oo moon oo book

You already know most of these words — you just haven't noticed the patterns yet

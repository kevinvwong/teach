Module 3

Long E — ee, ea, ey

UFLI Lesson 85

What sound do you hear in “see,” “eat,” and “key”? They all have the long E sound /e/ — but each one spells it differently.

The Rule for ee, ea, and ey

Three different vowel teams can make the long E sound. The good news: once you know the patterns, you can read hundreds of words.

ee — always says /e/

ee is the most reliable. It always says the long E sound. No exceptions.

see • tree • green • sleep • keep

ea — usually says /e/

ea most often says long E, but sometimes it says short E instead (like in “bread” or “head”). For now, try long E first.

eat • read • beach • team • leaf

ey — at the end of words

ey says /e/ when it comes at the end of a word. It's less common than ee or ea.

they • key • monkey • turkey • honey

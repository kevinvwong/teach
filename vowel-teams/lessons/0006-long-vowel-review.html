You've learned ai, ay, ee, ea, ey, oa, ow, oe, ie, and igh. Now let's bring them all together!

All the Long Vowel Teams

Here is a summary of every long vowel team you've learned so far. How many can you remember?

SoundSpellingWhere It GoesExamples
Long A /a/ ai Middle (rain) rain, train, wait, mail
Long A /a/ ay End (play) play, day, say, stay
Long E /e/ ee Middle or end (see) see, green, keep, sleep
Long E /e/ ea Middle (eat) eat, read, dream, beach
Long E /e/ ey End (key) key, honey, donkey
Long O /o/ oa Middle (boat) boat, road, soap, goal
Long O /o/ ow End (snow) snow, grow, slow, know
Long O /o/ oe End, rare (toe) toe, doe, hoe, foe
Long I /i/ igh Middle or end (light) light, night, high, bright
Long I /i/ ie End (pie) pie, tie, lie, die

10 spellings, 4 sounds. That's a lot of patterns! The good news: you already know every word in this chart. You just didn't know why they were spelled that way.

Quick Check

  1. Which vowel team says long A and is used at the end of a word like pl__? ay
  2. Which vowel team says long E and has a silent letter? (Hint: it's not ee or ea.) ey — the y is silent
  3. What sound does ow make in the word "snow"? Long O /o/
  4. In "light", which two letters are silent? gh
  5. How many different vowel team spellings have you learned in this unit? 10 — ai, ay, ee, ea, ey, oa, ow, oe, igh, ie

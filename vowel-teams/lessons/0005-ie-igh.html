Long I: ie and igh

UFLI Lesson 87

The long I sound is tricky — sometimes the gh is silent!

Two Ways to Spell /i/

The long I sound can be spelled igh or ie. One of them has a sneaky silent part. Can you spot it?

igh — silent gh

igh always says long /i/. The gh is silent — you only hear the long I sound. Look for igh in the middle or at the end of a word.

light night high bright

ie — at the end

ie at the end of a word usually says long /i/. It's short and sweet — just two letters.

pie tie lie die

Heads up: The letters ie can also say long E in the middle of a word (like field or piece). For now, we're focusing on ie at the end saying long /i/.

Quick Check

  1. In the word "light", which letters are silent? gh
  2. Does "pie" use igh or ie? ie
  3. Which spelling — igh or ie — has more letters? igh (three letters vs. two)

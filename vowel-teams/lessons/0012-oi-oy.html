oi and oy — The /oi/ Sound

UFLI Lesson 95

The /oi/ sound might be one of the noisiest sounds in English! Just think boy, coin, and joy.

oi in the Middle, oy at the End

The /oi/ sound follows a very simple rule. Once you learn it, you'll know exactly which spelling to use.

oi — In the Middle

Use oi when the /oi/ sound is in the middle of a word or syllable.

coin oil point voice boil

oy — At the End

Use oy when the /oi/ sound is at the end of a word or syllable.

boy toy joy enjoy annoy

This pattern is super reliable. Unlike some vowel teams, oi and oy almost never break this rule. If you hear /oi/ at the end of a word, it's oy. If you hear it in the middle, it's oi.

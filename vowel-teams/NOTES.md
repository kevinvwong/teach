# NOTES

## Design preferences
- Kid-friendly but not babyish (ages 9-11)
- Game-like activities, not drills
- Clear visual cues for vowel teams
- Color coding: highlight the vowel team in each word

## Curriculum
- 12 modules covering all major vowel teams
- Orton-Gillingham inspired scope and sequence
- Each lesson: Hook → Teach → Practice → Game → Check

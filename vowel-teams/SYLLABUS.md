# Vowel Teams — A Phonics Catch-Up Course

**Ages:** 9–11
**Format:** Self-paced interactive lessons, 15–20 minutes each
**Method:** UFLI-aligned scope and sequence (Lessons 84–97), Orton-Gillingham principles

## Course Description

This course covers the major vowel team patterns of English, following the research-backed sequence used by [UFLI Foundations](https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/foundations/) (Lessons 84–97). Each module teaches one or two patterns through a consistent five-phase lesson: **Hook → Teach → Practice → Game → Check**. The goal is automatic recognition and accurate spelling of vowel-team words.

---

## How to Use Each Lesson

Every lesson follows five phases:

| Phase | What happens | Approx. time |
|-------|--------------|-------------|
| **Hook** | A warm-up that surfaces the target sound — a riddle, a pattern-find, or a mini challenge | 2 min |
| **Teach** | Explicit rule instruction: what the team says, where it lives (middle vs. end), key exceptions | 5 min |
| **Practice** | Read-aloud, sort, or fill-in-the-blank with immediate feedback | 5 min |
| **Game** | A timed or competitive activity — matching, bingo, word-building, or nonsense-word decoding | 4 min |
| **Check** | 3–5 quick-review questions to confirm mastery before moving on | 2 min |

Students should complete modules **in order**. Each module assumes the previous ones are mastered.

---

## Module Sequence

UFLI Foundations teaches vowel teams in two units: **Long Vowel Teams** (Lessons 84–88) then **Other Vowel Teams** (Lessons 89–94), followed by **Diphthongs** (Lessons 95–97) and a final review.

### Unit 1: Long Vowel Teams (UFLI Lessons 84–88)

### Module 1: What's a Vowel Team?
- Introduction to the concept: two vowels working together to make one sound
- Vowel teams vs. digraphs (sh, ch, th) vs. blends (st, tr, pl)
- Pre-assessment: can you spot a vowel team?

### Module 2: ai and ay — The "A" Team (UFLI Lesson 84)
- ai goes in the **middle** of a word or syllable; ay goes at the **end**
- Keyword: **rain**, **play**
- Word list: rain, train, paint, wait, play, day, say, stay

### Module 3: ee, ea, ey — The "E" Team (UFLI Lesson 85)
- ee always says /ē/; ea can say /ē/ (eat) or /ĕ/ (bread); ey says /ē/ at word end (they, key)
- Keywords: **see**, **eat**, **key**
- Word list: see, tree, green, eat, read, beach, they, key

### Module 4: oa, ow, oe — The "O" Team (UFLI Lesson 86)
- oa in the **middle**; ow at the **end** (says /ō/); oe at the **end** (rare)
- Keywords: **boat**, **snow**, **toe**
- Word list: boat, road, soap, snow, grow, slow, toe, doe

### Module 5: ie, igh — The "I" Team (UFLI Lesson 87)
- igh — silent gh, always says long i; ie can say /ī/ at word end
- Keywords: **light**, **pie**
- Word list: light, night, high, right, pie, tie, lie

### Module 6: Long Vowel Teams Review (UFLI Lesson 88)
- Mixed practice sorting all long vowel teams
- Discriminate between confusable patterns
- Timed fluency drill

### Unit 2: Other Vowel Teams (UFLI Lessons 89–94)

### Module 7: oo (UFLI Lessons 89–90)
- Two sounds: /oo/ (food, moon) and /ŏŏ/ (book, look)
- No reliable rule — learned by word family
- Keywords: **moon**, **book**

### Module 8: ew, ui, ue (UFLI Lesson 91)
- ue at the **end** says /ū/ or /oo/ (blue, true); ew at the **end** (grew, flew); ui in the **middle** (fruit, suit)
- Keywords: **blue**, **fruit**, **grew**

### Module 9: Other Vowel Teams Review (UFLI Lesson 92)
- Mixed practice sorting oo, ew, ui, ue
- Real words vs. nonsense words

### Module 10: au, aw, augh (UFLI Lesson 93)
- au in the **middle**; aw at the **end** (or before l, n); augh (caught, taught)
- Keywords: **sauce**, **saw**, **caught**

### Module 11: ea /ĕ/, a /ŏ/ (UFLI Lesson 94)
- ea can say short e (bread, head, read-past-tense)
- a can say short o (want, wash, watch)
- Keywords: **bread**, **want**

### Unit 3: Diphthongs (UFLI Lessons 95–97)

### Module 12: oi, oy (UFLI Lesson 95)
- oi in the **middle**; oy at the **end**
- Keywords: **coin**, **boy**

### Module 13: ou, ow (UFLI Lesson 96)
- ou in the **middle**; ow at the **end**
- ow makes two sounds: /ow/ (cow) and /ō/ (snow) — learned in Module 4
- Keywords: **cloud**, **cow**

### Module 14: Vowel Teams & Diphthongs Final Review (UFLI Lesson 97)
- All vowel teams and diphthongs
- Read a connected passage with all patterns
- Post-assessment

Vowel Teams Glossary

Two vowels working together to make one sound. Here's every team you'll learn:

ai /ā/ — long A rain, train, mail, paint "A" says its name first
ay /ā/ — long A play, stay, day, maybe "Ay" comes at the end
ee /ē/ — long E see, tree, green, sleep Double E, double E, I see a tree
ea /ē/ — long E eat, read, beach, seat "Eat" your beach snack
igh /ī/ — long I high, light, night, right "I" + "gh" (silent guards)
oa /ō/ — long O boat, coat, road, soap "O" goes first in the boat
oe /ō/ — long O toe, doe, hoe, goes "O" and "E" go together
ue /ū/ or /oo/ — long U blue, glue, true, clue Blue glue sticks to you
ui /ū/ or /oo/ — long U fruit, suit, juice, build Fruit juice in a suit
oi /oy/ coin, soil, point, voice "Oi" — annoying sound!
oy /oy/ boy, toy, joy, annoy "Oy" comes at the end, boy!
ou /ow/ out, shout, cloud, mouse "Ou" — shout about the cloud
ow /ow/ or /ō/ cow, brown, down (cow sound)
snow, blow, slow (long O) "Ow" can say "ouch!" or "oh!"
au /aw/ sauce, haunt, launch, August "Au" — sauce in August
aw /aw/ saw, paw, draw, straw "Aw" — draw with your paw
oo /oo/ (long) or /ŏŏ/ (short) moon, food, school (long)
book, foot, good (short) "Oo" — food on the moon / a good book
